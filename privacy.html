Privacy policy

Workspace Assistant is used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account.

  • Google data accessed (Gmail, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Calendar) is used only to carry out the owner's own requests.
  • Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
